# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Our ideal customer is a mid-to-large organization where operations or IT owns a
recurring, measurable pain that our platform removes.

## Firmographics we look for

- **Company size:** 200+ employees. Above 500 is a strong signal; the scoring
  model awards the most points here.
- **Revenue:** $10M+ annual revenue, with $100M+ treated as enterprise.
- **Industries with strong fit:** Manufacturing, Healthcare, and FinTech.
  These segments have the compliance and scale pressures our platform targets.
- **Geography:** US, UK, and EU are fully supported today.

## Buying-role signals

- Senior titles (VP, Director, Chief, Head, Founder) indicate budget authority.
- High-intent inbound sources — webinar attendance and website form fills —
  correlate with faster cycles and should be prioritized.

## Who is NOT a fit

- Sub-50-employee companies with under $2M revenue.
- Cold-list contacts with no company attribution.
- Generic role titles that suggest an individual contributor without authority.

A lead that matches the firmographics AND shows a buying-role signal is a
route-to-sales candidate. Everything else is nurture or discard.
