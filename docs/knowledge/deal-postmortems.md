# Deal Postmortems (CONFIDENTIAL — admin only)

Internal loss analysis and pricing concessions. Not for distribution to the
sales floor: it contains discount floors and competitive intelligence.

## Vertex FinTech — LOST (Q4 2025, $30K)

We lost the Vertex FinTech platform deal after a three-month evaluation.
Primary reasons:

- **Price.** Vertex pushed hard on price; we offered a 22% discount off list
  and still lost. Their incumbent renewed at roughly 35% below our floor.
- **Missing SSO/SAML** at the time of evaluation was a hard blocker for their
  security team.
- **Slow proof-of-concept.** Our POC took five weeks; the competitor stood one
  up in eight days.

Lesson: for FinTech deals, lead with security (SSO/SAML) and a fast POC. Do not
exceed a 25% discount without VP approval — deeper concessions have not
recovered these deals historically.

## Karma Textiles — LOST (Q3 2025, $8K)

Too small to justify custom onboarding; churned in the pilot. Sub-scale
accounts like this should be nurtured, not routed to an AE.

## Pricing concession ladder (confidential)

- Standard: up to 10% off list, AE discretion.
- Up to 25% off list: manager approval.
- Beyond 25%: VP approval, and only for $100M+ revenue accounts.
